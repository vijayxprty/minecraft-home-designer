How to build
Click Add blocks, pick a block in Elements, then click where it goes.
Drag to look around · Scroll to zoom · 0 fits the build in view
Hold Space and drag to move the view
Click a block to select it · Shift+click grabs the whole row · Ctrl+A selects all · Del deletes
With Select, drag empty grass to box-select an area · Shift adds to it · 🏠 Home resets the view.
